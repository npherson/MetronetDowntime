How this is measured

  • An Uptime Kuma monitor on a Raspberry Pi pings a public address (8.8.4.4) once a minute. A failed ping marks the internet as down until the next successful one, so each start and end time can be off by up to about a minute.
  • A second monitor pings our own router. Outages where our router was also down within 3 minutes are left out, since those point to a problem inside the house rather than with Metronet. 30 outages were left out this way.
  • Outages of 1 minute or less are a single failed check and could be a dropped ping rather than a real outage, so they are shown separately.
